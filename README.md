# SQL-advent-calendar-solutions

SQL Advent Calendar by Interview Master is a fun, free 24-day SQL challenge that runs throughout December! Each day , you unlock a brand new SQL puzzle featuring adorable holiday-themed scenarios - think Santa's workshop databases, Grinch mischief logs, and penguin sleigh ride signups! 🎅🐧

The challenges are designed for everyone, from SQL beginners to pros, and tackle real-world problems like data analysis, filtering, joins, and window functions. Get stuck? No worries - there's an AI assistant ready to help, plus hints and explanations to guide you along the way.

Complete all 24 challenges by the end of December and you'll earn a certificate of completion to show off your SQL skills! It's the perfect way to level up your database knowledge while getting into the holiday spirit. 🎄✨
